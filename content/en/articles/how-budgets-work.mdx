## Why budget?

A budget turns intention into a limit. Instead of discovering at the end of the month where the money went, you decide how much you plan to spend in each area and track the difference.

## Step by step

1. Pick a scope: a category (e.g., Groceries), an account, or a label.
2. Set a limit for the period (monthly or annual).
3. Record transactions as usual.
4. Compare accumulated spending with the limit.

## Example

| Category | Limit | Spent | Status |
| -------- | ----- | ----- | ------ |
| Groceries | $800 | $650 | On track |
| Leisure   | $300 | $340 | Over budget |

## Tip

Budgets work best when the amount is realistic. Adjust the limit based on recent months instead of starting from an ideal number.
