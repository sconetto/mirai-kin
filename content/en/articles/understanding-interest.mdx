## Simple vs. compound interest

**Simple interest** applies only to the initial principal. **Compound interest** applies to the principal and to accumulated interest, growing faster.

## What it means in practice

- On a debt, compound interest increases the total cost quickly.
- On an investment, compound interest accelerates growth over the long term.

## Price and SAC

| Method | Payment | Interest | Amortization |
| ------ | ------- | -------- | ------------ |
| Price  | Constant | Decreasing | Growing |
| SAC    | Decreasing | Decreasing | Constant |

## Rule of thumb

The longer the term and the higher the rate, the bigger the effect of interest. Simulating scenarios before taking a loan avoids surprises.
