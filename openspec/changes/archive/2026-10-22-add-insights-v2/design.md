## Context

Insights are generated by `src/services/insights.ts` as deterministic, localized, acknowledgement-tracked types. Adding generators follows the existing pattern.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- `idle_cash`: flag liquid cash above a configurable threshold relative to spending.
- `net_worth_milestone`: flag crossing a round milestone.
- `spending_anomaly`: flag a month whose spend deviates statistically from its own trend.
- `subscription_audit`: summarize recurring subscriptions and flag possible duplicates or unused ones.

**Non-Goals:**

- Probabilistic or ML-based anomaly detection beyond a simple z-score/percentile rule.
- Automatic cancellation of subscriptions.

## Decisions

### 1. Thresholds

Use configurable thresholds with sensible defaults, stored in settings: idle-cash months-of-expenses floor, milestone step, and anomaly standard-deviation multiplier.

### 2. Anomaly method

Compute per-category or total monthly spend series, then flag a period whose value exceeds the trailing mean by N standard deviations. Deterministic and explainable.

### 3. Subscription audit

Depends on the subscriptions entity from `add-entities`. If absent, the generator is skipped rather than erroring.

### 4. Actions

Each insight links to the relevant screen (accounts, equity, subscriptions).
